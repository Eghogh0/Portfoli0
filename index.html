01 · About

An engineer’s discipline, a designer’s eye.

The short version of who I am.

I’m Eghogho — a Computer Science student at Pan-Atlantic University who treats every project like a product: designed, built, shipped, and measured.

My work lives at the intersection of engineering and visual design. I write the front end, but I think like a designer — the weight of a number on a dashboard, the rhythm of a page, the one colour that carries the whole thing. My capstone concept, Walka, is built on that belief: a fitness app that a real person would open every morning.

Off-screen, the discipline continues with a pencil. Drawing and graphic design are daily habits, not hobbies — and they feed the code every week.
